# Brief — Casa Escondida Anilao

Casa Escondida is a boutique **dive resort** in **Anilao, Batangas, Philippines**. It offers
**24 rooms** across four room types (Standard, Deluxe, Villa, Dorm), a dive centre serving
**35+ dive sites**, PADI courses, gear rental, and an on-site restaurant and bar.

- Company name: Casa Escondida Anilao
- Location: Anilao, Batangas, Philippines
- Room count: 24
- Dive site count: 35
- Currency: PHP
- Tone: warm, expert, understated.
